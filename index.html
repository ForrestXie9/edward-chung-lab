Overview

Led by Prof. Edward Chung, UMI Lab brings together researchers at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University to improve how cities move.

We develop methods for traffic prediction, transport network optimisation, digital twins, and autonomous mobility. Our work combines AI, data science, and transport modelling to support more efficient and sustainable transport systems.
